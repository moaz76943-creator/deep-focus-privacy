1. Scope and publisher

This Privacy Policy explains how Deep Focus: Study & Work Timer (the “App”, package com.deepfocus.study) handles information. The App is published under the name Hikmah Lab, a publisher name used by an individual developer and not a registered company or separate legal entity. Contact email: moaz76943@gmail.com.

This Policy applies to the App and direct support emails you send to us. It does not govern independent operating systems (Android), app stores (Google Play), or third-party providers that you choose to use.

2. Information stored on your device

The App is designed as a local-first study and productivity tool and does not require account registration. Data you create or use inside the App—including projects, tasks, notes, notebooks, calendar events, flashcards, decks, review history, goals, habits, focus sessions, reminders, settings, and related media—is stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database protected by a key stored using Android secure storage.

This encryption design helps protect local records at rest, but no local storage design can guarantee protection if your device, operating system, or user account is compromised.

Local diagnostic error log: Separately from the encrypted database, the App maintains a small, size-limited local diagnostic error log file on your device to record technical failure metadata (such as timestamp, error type, source, and limited stack frames). It is not uploaded automatically to developer servers.

3. Internet use and optional external assistant

The App does not operate developer-hosted user accounts or automatic cloud synchronization for your study records. The App uses network access (INTERNET) only for an optional external assistant feature and for Play Integrity attestation (device/app integrity checks with Google):

  • If you do not configure an external endpoint, the App does not send your study content over the network through this feature.
  • If you choose to configure an external HTTPS API endpoint (non-HTTPS URLs are rejected) and enter your own API key, the text you select to submit is sent directly from your device to your chosen endpoint, with your API key sent in the Authorization header, after required user consent.
  • Your API key is stored in Android secure storage and is excluded from backup files and diagnostic logs.
  • The external provider you choose processes your requests under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not operate, inspect, store, or control that external provider.

4. Microphone, voice typing and text-to-speech (TTS)

  • Flashcard voice recordings (RECORD_AUDIO): Requested only when you choose to record a voice clip for a flashcard. Audio is captured temporarily, read into the App’s encrypted database (card_media) with the temporary file deleted, included in encrypted backups you create, and never uploaded automatically to developer servers.
  • System voice typing: Invokes your device’s built-in Android speech-recognition interface. The App does not create a separate audio recording during this flow; voice processing is governed by your device’s speech-recognition provider (locally or remotely depending on device settings).
  • Text-to-speech (TTS): Passes the selected text to the Android TTS engine configured on your device. Depending on your chosen engine and voice pack, processing may occur locally or require network connectivity under that provider’s privacy policy.

5. Backups and ordinary exports

  • Automatic local backups: On Android, the App periodically checks for local backup eligibility (shortly after launch and when returning to the foreground) and creates an automatic encrypted backup inside the App’s private storage when at least 20 hours have elapsed since the previous backup, retaining up to the 7 most recent backups. These automatic backups are encrypted using a random password managed by the App and stored in Android secure storage, and remain locally on your device.
  • Manual password-encrypted backups: You may manually create a backup file protected with a password you choose, using Argon2id key derivation and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption, and save or share it outside the App’s private storage. Because you control the password, we cannot decrypt or recover a manual backup if you lose the password or if the file is corrupted or modified.
  • Ordinary exports (unencrypted): Standard exported documents and tables (such as CSV, Excel, Word, PDF, Markdown, or Text, depending on the feature) are not protected by backup encryption.
  • External files: Any backup or export saved or shared outside the App’s private storage is outside our control and is not automatically deleted when you uninstall the App.

6. Device permissions and system features

  • INTERNET: Optional user-configured external assistant and Play Integrity attestation with Google.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Optional flashcard voice recordings.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS & VIBRATE: Focus timer alerts and study reminders.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED & SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Restoring scheduled reminders after reboot; exact-alarm access is declared only up to Android API 32, while newer Android versions use inexact scheduling subject to system power management.

You can manage or revoke permissions at any time in Android system settings.

7. Support messages

If you email moaz76943@gmail.com for support or privacy inquiries, we receive your email address, message content, and any attachments you choose to include. We use this information only to respond, investigate technical issues, and comply with legal obligations.

8. Advertising, analytics and paid features

The current release does not include advertising SDKs (including Google Mobile Ads / AdMob), third-party analytics, remote crash-reporting services, cloud accounts, or active in-app subscriptions. We do not sell personal information. Play Integrity is used for optional device/app attestation with Google and does not collect study content. If future versions introduce advertising, paid features, or additional data practices, we will update this Policy and the Google Play Data Safety form accordingly.

9. Retention, deletion and your privacy rights

  • Encrypted database & automatic backups: Stored locally until deleted in the App, cleared via Android app storage settings, or removed by uninstalling the App.
  • Local diagnostic error log: Stored locally in a size-limited file and removed when clearing app storage or uninstalling the App.
  • External backups and exports: Remain wherever you saved them until you delete them manually.
  • Support emails: Retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and basic compliance records. You may request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence by emailing moaz76943@gmail.com.

Depending on applicable data protection laws in your place of residence, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent where applicable, and lodge a complaint with your competent data protection authority.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is a general study and productivity tool and is not directed specifically to children under the age requiring verifiable parental consent under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us personal information, please contact moaz76943@gmail.com for prompt review and deletion.

4. الميكروفون، الإملاء الصوتي، وتحويل النص إلى كلام (TTS)

  • تسجيلات البطاقات الصوتية (RECORD_AUDIO): يُطلب الإذن فقط عندما تختار تسجيل مقطع صوتي لبطاقة تعليمية. يُلتقط الصوت مؤقتاً ثم يُقرأ ويُحفظ داخل قاعدة البيانات المشفرة للتطبيق (card_media) مع حذف الملف المؤقت، ويدخل ضمن النسخ الاحتياطية المشفرة التي تنشئها، ولا يُرفع تلقائياً إلى خوادم المطوّر.

  • تحويل النص إلى كلام (TTS): يمرّر النص المختار إلى محرك Android TTS المضبوط على جهازك. وبحسب المحرك وحزمة الصوت المختارة، قد تتم المعالجة محلياً أو تتطلب اتصالاً بالشبكة وفق سياسة خصوصية ذلك المزوّد.

  • النسخ الاحتياطية المحلية التلقائية: على نظام Android، يفحص التطبيق دورياً أهلية النسخ الاحتياطي المحلي (بعد وقت قصير من التشغيل وعند العودة إلى الواجهة) وينشئ نسخة احتياطية مشفرة تلقائية داخل مساحة التطبيق الخاصة عند مرور 20 ساعة على الأقل منذ النسخة السابقة، مع الاحتفاظ بما يصل إلى آخر 7 نسخ. تُشفّر هذه النسخ التلقائية بكلمة مرور عشوائية يديرها التطبيق ويحفظها في التخزين الآمن في Android، وتبقى محلياً على جهازك.
  • النسخ الاحتياطية اليدوية المشفرة بكلمة مرور: يمكنك إنشاء ملف نسخة احتياطية يدوياً محمي بكلمة مرور تختارها أنت باستخدام اشتقاق المفاتيح Argon2id والتشفير الموثّق AES-256-GCM، وحفظه أو مشاركته خارج مساحة التطبيق الخاصة. ونظراً لأنك وحدك تتحكم بكلمة المرور، لا يمكننا فك تشفير النسخة اليدوية أو استعادتها إذا فقدت كلمة المرور أو إذا تلف الملف أو عُدّل.
  • ملفات التصدير العادية (غير مشفرة): المستندات والجداول المصدّرة العادية (مثل CSV أو Excel أو Word أو PDF أو Markdown أو Text بحسب الميزة) غير محمية بتشفير النسخ الاحتياطية.
  • الملفات الخارجية: أي نسخة احتياطية أو ملف مصدّر تحفظه أو تشاركه خارج مساحة التطبيق الخاصة يخرج عن سيطرتنا ولا يُحذف تلقائياً عند إزالة تثبيت التطبيق.
