1. Built for Your Personal Workspace

Deep Focus is designed so your tasks, notes, flashcards, schedules, and study preferences stay on your own device by default. You stay in full control of what you create, export, or share.

2. Optional System & External Features

If you choose to use device features (like Text-to-Speech voices) or optional external tools you configure yourself, those services follow their own terms and privacy policies. We simply provide the app interface and are not responsible for external providers.

3. Backups & Device Care

You can export encrypted backups whenever you like. Because your data and encryption passwords stay with you, please keep a safe copy of your backups and passwords—we cannot access or recover lost local data.

Deep Focus is provided as a helpful productivity tool in its current form ("as available"). While we continuously work to improve stability and features, we cannot guarantee uninterrupted operation on every device or be held responsible for unexpected data loss or technical issues.
